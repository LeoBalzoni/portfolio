# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, served by the same material (confirmed by Leonardo, 2026-11-21):

- Engineering peers and the wider community, who arrive for a specific piece of writing or a talk write-up and may stay to read more.
- Hiring managers and recruiters evaluating him for senior roles, who want evidence of judgement and a way to reach him.

The site is writing-led: the writing and work stories lead, and hiring evidence follows from them.

## Product Purpose

Leonardo Balzoni's personal home on the web. It publishes his writing (engineering posts, talk write-ups), documents his work at Nexthink in enough depth to show how he thinks, and shows the side projects he builds for fun. Success is a visitor reading at least one piece in full and knowing how to contact him.

## Positioning

The work stories are drawn from his actual commit and pull-request history across roughly forty repositories over five years, so the claims are specific and countable. He works the full vertical on one data platform: Kafka extraction, Spark pipelines, serving APIs, an MCP server for LLM agents, the UI, and the GitOps delivery under all of it. He is also the person who gave the company-wide talk "How to survive without going insane in the AI era".

## Operating Context

- Static export (Next.js `output: "export"`) deployed to GitHub Pages under the `/portfolio` base path by a workflow that runs on every push to `main`.
- Posts are MDX files in `content/blog/`.
- Readers arrive on phones from shared links as often as on desktops.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No backend, no CMS, no contact form: GitHub and LinkedIn are the contact routes.
- One light theme. The earlier dark and light toggle was removed in the November 2026 redesign.
- Disclosure level for Nexthink work: full detail, including repository and internal project names (Leonardo's decision, 2026-11-21; he takes responsibility for clearing it with Nexthink).
- The talk post is text, with a slot for a video link he will supply once the recording is hosted publicly.
- The contact email shown on the site is his personal address, as it was in the previous footer.
- Undecided: a résumé PDF, a custom domain.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Leonardo Balzoni. Based in Lausanne, software engineer at Nexthink on the Cloud Intelligence team.
- No visual element of the previous site is binding; the 3D Da Vinci bust and the "Not this Leonardo" line may be replaced.
- Voice in existing writing: first person, plain, dry, sceptical of hype, generous with what went wrong.

## Product Principles

1. Writing first: every section should lead a visitor toward something worth reading.
2. Specific over impressive: dates, counts and trade-offs from the real history, including the parts that went wrong.
3. Work and play are the same person: the party games sit beside the platform work, not in a footnote.
4. Nothing a reader must take on trust that could instead be shown.
